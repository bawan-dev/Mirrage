# Frame Design

The frame should make Mirrage look finished while still allowing airflow and
maintenance.

## Options

| Frame type | Pros | Risks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Simple wooden frame | Affordable, easy to build, easy to modify | Finish quality depends on workmanship |
| Deep shadow-box frame | Good depth for display and cables | Can become bulky |
| Aluminium profile frame | Clean, strong, modular | More expensive, needs careful planning |
| Cabinet-style build | Best service access and structure | More work and depth |
| Modified mirror frame | Can look good quickly | May not have enough depth or strength |

## Recommendation

Recommended first build: **deep wooden shadow-box frame with removable back
panel**.

Why:

- practical with normal tools
- can hide display depth
- allows top/bottom ventilation
- can include a removable service back
- easier to adjust than aluminium
- can be painted or finished cleanly

## Design Requirements

- enough depth for display, cables, and air gap
- removable back panel
- access to mini PC power button and USB ports
- cable exit with strain relief
- top and bottom ventilation
- strong wall mounting points
- mirror material supported evenly
- no pressure points on glass or acrylic

## Dimensions

Do not invent final dimensions before the display and mirror material are chosen.

Measure:

- display width and height
- display depth including cables
- VESA mount clearance
- mirror material thickness
- required air gap
- speaker and microphone placement
- cable exit path

Final dimensions should be based on the actual parts.

## Appearance

Aim for:

- matte black or dark neutral inner surfaces
- clean front edge
- no visible loose cables
- no obvious electronics from normal viewing distance
- service access hidden on the back, not impossible to reach
