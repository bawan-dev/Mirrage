# Mirror Material

The mirror material decides whether Mirrage feels like a mirror or just a screen
behind tinted plastic.

Every material reduces display brightness. Test a sample over the chosen display
before final assembly.

## Options

| Material | Pros | Risks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two-way acrylic mirror | Light, easier to cut, safer to handle | Scratches easily, can flex, may look less premium |
| Two-way glass mirror | Better finish, more scratch-resistant, flatter | Heavy, fragile, harder to mount, shipping risk |
| Smart mirror glass | Designed for displays, good finish | Expensive, availability varies |
| Observation mirror glass | Strong mirror effect | Can be too dark for display visibility |
| Reflective film on glass | Cheap for experiments | Bubbles, uneven finish, often looks poor |

## Comparison

| Criteria | Acrylic | Glass | Smart mirror glass | Film |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reflectivity | Good | Very good | Good to excellent | Varies |
| Display visibility | Good if tint is right | Good if tint is right | Usually best | Often inconsistent |
| Durability | Scratches easily | Better surface durability | Good | Depends on application |
| Weight | Low | High | Medium/high | Depends on glass |
| Cost | Moderate | Higher | Higher | Low |
| Mounting | Easier | Harder | Harder | Medium |
| Cleaning | Needs care | Easier | Easier | Easy to damage |
| Safety | Less dangerous if cracked | Breakage risk | Breakage risk | Depends on glass |

## Recommendation

First test: **two-way acrylic mirror sample**.

First wall build: **two-way acrylic or smart mirror glass after brightness test**.

Use acrylic if:

- keeping weight down matters
- you want easier cutting and safer handling
- this is the first frame build

Use glass if:

- the final size is confirmed
- wall mounting is strong enough
- you want a more premium finish
- you can safely handle and mount the weight

## Practical Notes

- Avoid cheap reflective film for the final build unless a test panel looks
  genuinely good.
- Acrylic scratches, so clean it with a soft cloth and appropriate cleaner.
- Glass is heavier and can break, so wall mounting and frame strength matter.
- Darker mirror material improves reflection but makes the display dimmer.
- Brighter display output helps, but more brightness also means more heat.

## Required Test

Before frame construction:

1. Put the mirror sample over the display.
2. Show Mirrage Mirror Mode with black background and white text.
3. Test daytime and nighttime room light.
4. Check reflection quality with the screen on and off.
5. Decide whether the display brightness is acceptable.

Do not order final-size mirror material until this test passes.
