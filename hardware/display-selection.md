# Display Selection

The display is the most important first hardware decision because it controls
brightness, frame depth, heat, power use, and the final size of the mirror.

## Options

| Option | Pros | Risks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 24 inch monitor | Affordable, easy to mount, lower heat, easier frame | May feel small for a wall mirror |
| 27 inch monitor | Good balance of size, cost, availability, and readability | Needs a deeper frame than a portable panel |
| 32 inch monitor | Strong visual presence, useful at distance | Heavier, hotter, more expensive, larger frame |
| Portable display | Thin, light, lower power | Smaller, often lower brightness, awkward mounting |
| TV panel | Large and available used | More heat, thicker electronics, less serviceable |
| Repurposed monitor | Cheapest way to test | Unknown brightness, thickness, age, mounting |
| Commercial display panel | Bright and durable | Expensive, may be overkill for v1 |

## Comparison

| Criteria | 24 inch monitor | 27 inch monitor | 32 inch monitor | Portable display | TV panel |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brightness | Good if IPS/office monitor | Good if chosen carefully | Good but varies | Often weaker | Varies |
| Viewing angles | Good with IPS | Good with IPS | Good with IPS/VA tradeoffs | Varies | Varies |
| Thickness | Medium | Medium | Medium/deep | Thin | Often deep |
| Heat | Lower | Moderate | Higher | Low | Higher |
| Power | Lower | Moderate | Higher | Low | Higher |
| VESA mount | Common | Common | Common | Less common | Common |
| Bezel size | Usually fine | Usually fine | Usually fine | Small | Can be large |
| Cost | Lower | Moderate | Higher | Moderate | Varies |
| Serviceability | Good | Good | Good | Mixed | Mixed |

## Recommendation

Recommended first-build size: **27 inch IPS monitor with VESA mounting**.

Why:

- large enough to feel like a real mirror product
- still practical to frame and wall mount
- easy to source
- easier to service than a laptop panel
- enough room for large ambient typography
- works with standard HDMI/DisplayPort and normal power

## Build Tiers

| Tier | Display path | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budget | Existing or used 24-27 inch monitor | Best for visibility testing before spending money |
| Recommended | 27 inch IPS monitor, 1080p or 1440p, VESA mount | Best first wall-mounted build |
| Premium | Bright commercial display or high-quality 32 inch monitor | Only after frame, heat, and mirror tests are proven |

## What To Test Before Committing

- white text through mirror material in daytime room light
- black background reflection quality
- off-axis readability
- heat after 1 hour and 4 hours
- cable clearance behind the display
- VESA mount compatibility
- whether buttons and ports remain reachable

Do not choose a display only because it looks impressive on paper. Brightness,
mounting, thickness, and heat matter more for the first physical mirror.
